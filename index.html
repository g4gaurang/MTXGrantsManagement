Built for grantors

A grants operating layer—not another disconnected portal.

Grant programs carry distinct authority, funding rules, application models, reporting schedules, and oversight duties. MTX Gov Grants provides a reusable product core that agencies configure around those differences.

01
⌘

Fragmented lifecycle records

Applications, scores, agreements, reports, payments, and monitoring evidence often sit in separate tools and inboxes.

Product responseA connected grant record from opportunity through closeout.
02
⇄

Program and fiscal handoffs

Program approval, budget checks, payment authorization, ERP posting, and reconciliation can be difficult to follow.

Product responseShared workflow with distinct program and fiscal controls.
03
◎

Post-award visibility gaps

Leadership needs to see not only what was awarded, but whether grantees are reporting, spending, and delivering results.

Product responseFinancial, performance, risk, and monitoring views in context.

Product capabilities

Twelve modules grounded in grants administration.

Adopt the modules a program needs while keeping a common grant, organization, award, financial, performance, and oversight record.

01
▦

Program & Funding Setup

Program types, funding sources, appropriations, match requirements, award ranges, eligibility rules, calendars, roles, and versioned configuration.

Configure multiple programs and fiscal periods without hard-coding one operating model.

02
◫

Opportunities & Solicitation

Funding opportunity content, application packages, program guidance, eligibility requirements, publication, amendments, questions, and notices.

Support competitive, formula, continuation, and other agency-configured opportunity models.

03
✎

Applications & Eligibility

Guided forms, save and resume, collaborators, budgets, documents, certifications, submission validation, receipts, and eligibility screening.

Give applicants program-specific guidance while retaining submitted versions and attestations.

04
★

Evaluation & Selection

Reviewer pools, assignments, conflicts and recusals, weighted criteria, individual and panel reviews, clarifications, recommendations, and approval history.

Authorized personnel retain eligibility determinations, selection authority, and award decisions.

05
◇

Awards & Agreements

Award notices, packages, agreements, terms, funding periods, budgets, measures, schedules, special conditions, acknowledgement, and award history.

Carry approved application data into a governed award record without repeated entry.

06
$
Core financial capability

Budgets, Payments & Reconciliation

Funding sources, award amounts, budget categories, revisions, obligations, advances, reimbursements, approvals, payment status, adjustments, recoveries, and closeout balances.

Exchange authorized transactions and status with the agency ERP or payment service while preserving the grant context.

07
↻

Amendments & Change Control

Budget revisions, scope and schedule changes, extension requests, agreement amendments, routing, approvals, effective dates, and linked versions.

Keep the original award and each approved change visible in sequence.

08
⌁

Performance & Deliverables

Goals, measures, milestones, outcomes, reporting schedules, programmatic and financial reports, evidence, exceptions, and follow-up.

Relate funded activity and reported results to the terms of each award.

09
⌖

Monitoring, Risk & Compliance

Risk assessments, monitoring plans, desk reviews, site visits, findings, special conditions, corrective actions, evidence, resolution, and audit history.

Configure oversight to funding-source and program requirements, including applicable Uniform Guidance controls.

10
✓

Closeout & Records

Final reports, deliverable completion, final reconciliation, outstanding obligations, property or record checks, closeout review, retention, and reopening history.

Coordinate program and fiscal completion before recording the final closeout decision.

11
▥

Reporting, Analytics & Transparency

Portfolio, program, geography, funding, evaluation, payment, performance, risk, monitoring, and closeout views with governed exports and reports.

Offer role-specific operational views and share approved data with public transparency services when required.

12
⎔

Integration & Product Administration

APIs, events, secure batch, identity, organization data, document services, notifications, ERP exchange, configuration, access, and release controls.

Connect selected systems while maintaining governed boundaries and transaction traceability.

Configured to the authority

Support applicable grant requirements without treating every program the same.

For federal awards and pass-through activity, agencies can configure relevant requirements from 2 CFR Part 200 and program-specific terms into risk reviews, monitoring, reporting, financial controls, records, and closeout workflows. The agency determines which requirements apply to each funding source and award.
