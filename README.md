# MTX Gov Grants

Product landing-page prototype for a configurable, grantor-side government grants management platform.

## Local preview

```shell
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Open `http://localhost:4173`.

## Published site

The GitHub Pages workflow publishes the `main` branch to:

`https://g4gaurang.github.io/MTXGrantsManagement/`

## Product scope

* Program and funding setup
* Funding opportunities and applications
* Evaluation and award
* Budgets, payments, and reconciliation
* Performance and deliverables
* Monitoring, risk, and corrective actions
* Closeout and records
* Analytics, assistive AI, and agency integrations

The dashboard figures are illustrative. Named proof points come from the MTX Gov Grants Management product artifact supplied for this prototype and should receive public-use approval before production publication.

## Research basis

The product narrative uses grantor terminology and lifecycle guidance from Grants.gov, SAM.gov, and 2 CFR Part 200. It also draws on public grants product patterns from Deloitte and CGI without reproducing competitor language.
